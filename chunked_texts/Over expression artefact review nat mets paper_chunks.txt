The transience of transient overexpression
Toby J Gibson, Markus Seiler & Reiner A Veitia

transfected cells. However, overexpression can violate balanced gene dosage, affecting protein folding,
complex assembly and downstream regulation. To avoid these problems, genome engineering technologies
now enable the generation of stable cell lines expressing modified proteins at (almost) native levels.
